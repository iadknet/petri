Creature windows

Every reading below comes from the creature windows the run recorded in the telemetry stack: consecutive ticks of one creature, with what it sensed in its eight neighboring cells, what its brain did, what it chose and what the world applied. Nothing new is measured.

Senses → movement

Where the first move of a tick lands, against chance

For each tick with a move, did the creature step toward a neighbor holding food, fruit, a barrier or another creature? Chance is the share of its eight neighbors holding that thing at the moment it sensed them. Ticks where none or all eight neighbors held it are left out, because there was no choice to make. A tick can hold several actions; only its first move starts from the sensed cell, so only that move is read here.

What the world did with those moves

Eating on the sensed cell, against food underfoot

Counts an Eat only when it comes before the tick's first move, because a later Eat lands on another cell. A creature that used its food sense would eat far more often when food is underfoot.

Ticks inside one window are not independent: a creature pressed against a wall bumps it on every tick. Read the ratios as a description of this sample, not as a test, and check the counts.

Behavior

Fixed programs

A window whose action sequence repeats with a period of one to three ticks is running the same program whatever it senses. A window that only ever moves one way has no steering.
