# T13 — Neutral Module Recruitment

**Status**: In Progress
**Last updated**: 2026-09-14
**Master**: [Program Roadmap](../roadmap.md)

## Goal

New brain tissue can join a working controller without destroying its behavior,
acquire useful computation through viable mutations, and persist when its
contribution pays for its cost. Observe that progression by backend and lineage
so silent scaffold, mutation starvation, inaccessible activation and unsuccessful
selection can be distinguished before changing the substrate.

## Track Success Criteria

- [x] Stored readings distinguish module creation, mutation opportunity, input/effect acquisition, execution, behavioral contribution, useful contribution, retention and loss, with backend, lineage depth, denominators and observation limits.
- [ ] A replicated recruitment baseline separates variation between lineages from offspring sampling, and drift from selection; every repair has a comparable reading without replacing historical results.
- [ ] Adding modules that cannot accept an operator does not suppress that operator on valid targets elsewhere; growth and connection operations still reach silent scaffold.
- [ ] Wired Graph effects can execute without an unrelated compute node, while truly inert Graph modules remain pass-through and every applied effect obeys the existing routing, state and energy contracts.
- [x] Both Graph and VM modules have demonstrated short, viable mutation paths from neutral creation to useful contribution in named tasks, preserving the incumbent behavior during the neutral steps.
- [ ] A bounded qualification records discovery and retention of newly recruited modules under selection, alongside damage, maintenance and execution cost; unresolved recruitment gaps remain explicit and do not become cognition claims.

## Executable Features

- [x] **T13.F01 — Module Recruitment Observability** — Depends on: T11.F14, T11.F16
  - Goal: Observe a new brain module from its creation through mutation, expression, contribution and loss, so each closure shows where recruitment advances or stalls.
- [x] **T13.F02 — Recruitment Paths and Replicated Baseline** — Depends on: T13.F01, T11.F05, T11.F08
  - Goal: Measure how often new Graph and VM modules acquire useful behavior through viable steps, separating lineage variation, dormant preparation and selection from mere growth.
- [x] **T13.F03 — Mutation Target Applicability** — Depends on: T13.F02
  - Goal: Point mutations alter existing molecular sites: refinement reaches a module with a suitable site while silent tissue keeps its own opportunities to grow and connect.
- [x] **T13.F04 — Direct Graph Effect Activation** — Depends on: T13.F02
  - Goal: Direct synaptic transmission: a wired signal can affect a controller without first growing an unrelated processing cell, while unwired tissue preserves the incumbent decision.
- [ ] **T13.F05 — Function-Preserving Module Recruitment** — Depends on: T13.F03, T13.F04, T11.F18
  - Goal: Gene duplication and circuit growth: new Graph and VM tissue can extend working computation through short viable mutation paths and then diverge into a useful contribution.
- [ ] **T13.F06 — Recruitment and Retention Qualification** — Depends on: T13.F05, T03.F08
  - Goal: Measure whether newly recruited tissue is discovered and retained because it helps a creature, including the cost of carrying it and its response to a changed task.

## Notes for AI Agents

- Evidence and alternatives are in the [recruitment research note](../strategy/neutral-module-recruitment-research-2026-09-08.md), reviewed 2026-09-08. It distinguishes inspected code paths, T11.F18 observations and its recorded closure exception and literature results. The standing contract is accessible recruitment with measured retention, not equal backend populations or a larger changed-birth score.
- Ownership. T13 owns the progression from neutral module growth to recruitment, its observations, operator-target applicability and the activation/refinement repairs listed here. T11 retains the general node-type contract, representation, clocks, duplication guarantees, learned-state inheritance and mutation-rate calibration. T13 edits those existing implementations and reference specs; it introduces no alternate runtime or mutation engine. T11.F10 consumes the qualified substrate; T11.F13 compares supply policies using T13's readings. T08 retains general lineage/heredity infrastructure, T09 cognitive qualification, and T03.F08 metabolic carrying cost. Existing feature IDs and completed contracts are retained.
- Sequence. F01 and F02 establish observation and a baseline before F03/F04 change behavior; F05 uses those repairs and T11.F18's backend-neutral creation; F06 qualifies the combined substrate before T11.F10. F01/F02 can start without T11.F18: explicit Graph/VM constructed fixtures are labeled as such, while production birth readings report whatever the current constructor actually creates. T11.F18 closed while this track was being drafted, with its measured miss and feature-specific user exception recorded in its spec. This track preserves that decision and the standing floor; it adds no retrospective T11.F18 requirement. Existing in-flight work is not reassigned.
- **F01 scope.** Extend existing neighborhood/mesh/drift records and the goal report, then show compact readings in `docs/progress.md` and its per-feature JSON. Record actual mutation attempts, applicable targets, applied edits and local failures by backend/operator, plus cohort transitions for newly introduced modules. Distinguish a module that was never selected, was selected but had no applicable site, changed only internally, began producing effects, or affected behavior. Keep per-lineage counts rather than only pooled totals. Use bounded observation provenance to distinguish a founder module, new module and descendant copy; define identity across deletion/copy explicitly. New cohort tracking belongs in the observation harness, not an unbounded production genealogy. Reuse T11.F18 backend fields when present. Unobserved fields are unmeasured, not zero.
- **F01 semantics and limits.** A node's dispatch, an output/state effect, a battery-sensitive contribution and a useful contribution are separate facts. Use the existing knockout loop for battery contribution; require a paired named-task or ecological outcome for usefulness. Include queue payloads, route effects, shared memory and temporal state in relevant fixtures. Track time/depth to first acquisition, first contribution and retention, with deletion, lineage extinction and end-of-observation distinguished; never drop non-recruiters from the denominator. Additive observation must not consume production RNG, select survivors or change execution. Budget the bounded cohort reading in the spec before implementation within existing closure limits; no new dashboard, database, profile or campaign runner.
- **F02 scope.** Reuse T11.F05's task/runtime seams and T11.F08's split/copy fixtures. Establish at least one explicit short path per backend from inert tissue through preparation to an observable effect, and test whether that effect can help on a named existing reactive task; add a changed-task treatment using existing sensors/actions, comparing matched histories with and without dormant preparation. Constructed paths prove possibility, not evolutionary discovery. Then measure production mutation discovery on blank and copy/split starting forms with matched incumbent behavior, separately under unconditional drift and bounded selection. Retain live/dead parent status, module count, costs and backend-specific exposure; report nulls and exact mutations along observed successful paths. Do not require both random backend choices to appear in an invariant test.
- **F02 comparison contract.** Predeclare independent lineage batches, offspring counts, task outcomes, retention horizon, practical-loss margin and wall budget in its just-in-time spec. Use paired parents where possible; if an extra RNG draw changes walk histories, label the comparison and use a VM-only sham-draw arm to investigate that confound, not to seek a passing seed. Analyze variation across lineages and preserve sibling outcomes. Record confidence/uncertainty and insufficient exposure honestly. A baseline may close with measured capability gaps assigned to F03-F05; it cannot claim those gaps repaired. Any proposed change to an existing acceptance rule is explicit and prospective, with historical readings and original decisions preserved.
- **F03 scope.** Select from the targets on which the chosen operator can apply, then apply the existing executed/reachable bias within that set. A local failure must not imply global operator unavailability. Preserve domain shares, configured operator weights, requested-event counts and residual opportunities for scaffold; changed applied mixes are measured consequences of the repair. Start with Graph edge/refinement operations and audit VM/input-reference operations only for the same defect. Prefer existing enum dispatch and local applicability helpers, sharing the predicate with application rather than creating a generic mutation framework. Prove with fixtures/properties that padding a genome with operator-inapplicable modules cannot erase a valid target, growth still reaches blank modules, and genuinely unavailable operators skip atomically. This changes mutation semantics and requires TDD, reference updates and full feature checks.
- **F04 scope.** Replace compute-node count as the proxy for whether a Graph has effects to execute. Direct input/shared-memory edges to value, route, memory and action surfaces follow existing effect semantics even with no internal compute node. Preserve truly unwired blank pass-through, successor execution, once-per-world-tick state, queues and budget exhaustion behavior. Apply the same effect ordering to zero- and nonzero-compute forms; define actual work/energy charges in the spec so removing a dummy compute node cannot create an unaccounted free-work path. Test direct source-to-effect fixtures beside inert Graphs and stateful graphs. Do not add a dummy node, new sensor, second effects engine or hidden activation flag.
- **F05 scope.** Use F02's paths to qualify and, where necessary, repair the existing safe split, copy/diverge and connection operators for both backends. Prefer already connected function-preserving structure over an additional catalog of blank templates. A path must retain incumbent behavior through its neutral steps and permit a subsequent bounded connection/parameter edit to expose a useful effect; memory/queue interference and real charges remain part of the contract. The spec fixes the supported fixture family and maximum path length from the baseline before changing operators. If existing paths already satisfy it, strengthen their regression coverage and record that no new production operator was needed. New code must address a demonstrated missing transition; no authored controller, novelty reward, global speciation, fan-out or guarantee that every neutral node becomes useful.
- **F06 scope.** Repeat the fixed F02 comparison on the integrated substrate and follow cohorts under selection with production carrying/execution costs. Report probability and time to first useful contribution, retention over the declared horizon, loss/censoring and paired task performance; a higher silent or changed fraction alone cannot pass usefulness. Include the unchanged-task and changed-task readings, then the ordinary goal-profile ecological reading, clearly separating assay selection from production ecology. Explicit fixtures must demonstrate both backends' viable useful paths. Stochastic nulls are reported as measured limits, not proof of impossibility or a reason to raise mutation rates; unresolved implementation failures block the responsible feature. The qualification provides T11.F10 and T11.F13 with exposure and retention evidence, not a universal emergence claim.
- Supply policy stays with **T11.F13**. Its existing per-node-plus-carrying-cost and active-target reference arms gain a comparison that separates opportunities for refinement from opportunities for dormant preparation. Match expected requested supply where possible, and report mismatches when growth scales it. Structural activity, recent dispatch and battery contribution are not interchangeable selectors. Do not use the observation battery as a mutation oracle, retry until behavior changes, prune every silent module, or add a second rate-calibration feature here.
- Verification and closure use `docs/workflow.md`; feature specs stay flat and are written just in time. Observation changes prove isolation and truthful aggregation; behavior changes use TDD and relevant property tests, viability first, fresh mutation evidence and the existing gate/goal reports. Performance and observation caps, previously declared floors and historical reports are not weakened by this roadmap edit. These are bounded engineering features, not the deferred confirmatory campaign phase.
