`A_scalar` gates its `Eat` vote on fruit being the only food: with grass here
the founder already eats once, and an ungated vote made the controller eat
twice (a competence violation the fixture caught), so only the type changes.
The `A_vector` gate is the four cardinal ring cells and the here cell:
nearest food on a diagonal ring cell opens it and steers on both axes, and
barriers on the diagonals never hide a cardinal ring cell (checked through
production visibility). Limitation: the area summary reports the nearest
visible food only, so a diagonal ring cell occluded by two orthogonal
barriers is simply absent and the controller steers toward the next nearest
food; the founder does not read diagonal ring cells either.

## Assay bookkeeping

Births are creatures alive at a tick's end and absent at the previous tick's
end, by inherited lineage arm; a newborn killed within its birth tick has no
observable parent without a production seam and counts in
`unattributed_births` (`births_total` = every arm's births + unattributed);
each replicate row stores both.
Replicate lines reach the raw record in world/replicate order. The run is
marked incomplete, with its stop reason, when the wall cap passes during or
after the replicates or the summary exceeds 300 KB. Exposure draws one
`SmallRng` per sampling tick and advances it through the six `A`/`Z` arms in
arm order.

## Funnel (goal-profile `input_use`): where input use stops

Generated by a script over the committed goal summary's
`deterministic.goal_indicators.cases[].input_use.cohorts[].rows`, not by
hand. Per world × cohort × family (food families keep their type, e.g.
`FoodHere:1`), the cells are parent counts at `declared`, `connected`,
`executed` and `causal`; **Stops at** names the first stage whose count
falls below half the previous stage's, or `none`. These are adjacent count
comparisons, not nested attrition: `connected` and `executed` are counted
independently, so `executed` can exceed `connected`. Retention is not a
stage here (it counts parent-channel × child pairs, not parents).

Rows are stored per channel. `declared` is a family count (the same on
every channel row); a multi-channel family's count at a later stage is the
union over its channels, which the summary does not store (the per-parent
sets exist only transiently in the funnel before the cohort fold). Those
cells show bounds `max over channels – min(sum over channels, declared,
parents)`. Where the bounds cannot decide the half-predecessor test, the
cell reads `unresolved:` followed by the candidate stages (conservative;
their joint feasibility is not established). Exact resolution needs the
family unions stored in the block. `UpstreamSlot` declares per slot
(channel = slot), so each slot is a unit and one row per cohort groups the
slots by stopping stage.

Shared memory (`SharedMemory`, `SharedMemoryPrevious`) has no `declared` and
no `causal` stage: causal use and retention are not measured for it (`n/a`,
not zero), and only `connected` → `executed` is tested. The founder cohorts
contain no shared-memory rows; nonzero connection and execution appear only
in the drift and selected cohorts.

**Headline.**

- Founder (identical in all three worlds): every family reaches causal use
  except `NeighborOccupiedRing` (declared, never connected: stops at
  connected) and `ActionQueue` (executed, never causal: stops at causal).
- Drift (Orchards and Confluence rows are identical; Canyon country
  differs): use mostly stops at causal. Families are declared, connected
  and executed but ablating them rarely moves the committed action;
  `NeighborBarrierRing` (Orchards, Confluence), `NearbyCreatureCore`,
  `NearbyCreatureVitals` (Orchards, Confluence), `AreaOccupancySummary`
  (Orchards, Confluence), `NearbyCreatureIdentity` and `PreviousOutcome`
  (Canyon) stop at connected. Only `FoodHere:0` and upstream slot 2
  resolve as passing in Orchards and Confluence; nothing resolves as passing
  in Canyon country. Unresolved cells are multi-channel families (ring, area,
  nearby-creature, vote, outcome and shared-memory) whose channel bounds
  straddle the half line.
- Selected: the founder's own families (`FoodHere:0`, `NeighborFoodRing:0`,
  `AgeTicks`, `EnergyCurrent`) pass in every world, as does `ActionQueue`
  except in Canyon country (stops at causal, 9 of 20). The founder's
  never-connected `NeighborOccupiedRing` still stops at connected, and the
  families the selected lineages newly declare (nearby-creature, area and
  vote families) mostly stop at connected. The second food type stops at
  causal in Confluence (`FoodHere:1` 8 → 3) and passes in Orchards (4 → 2).
