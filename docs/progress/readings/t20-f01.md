## Controller constants (fixed before the pilot)

Every controller is the canonical founder plus one reference appended to the
vote node (node 1, reference index 7) and the Graph structure below; `Z_k`
sets every authored edge weight to 0.0
(`crates/v3-core/src/neighborhood/opportunity/controllers.rs`).

| Arm | Reference | Authored channels | Structure | Constants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `A_ring` | `NeighborBarrierRing` | 0, 2, 4, 6 | `barrier[d] → Move(d)` for the four cardinal moves: 4 edges | weight −2.0 |
| `A_vector` | `AreaFoodSummary(0)` | 3 (dx), 4 (dy), 5 (dist) | `far = Threshold(0.17)(dist)`; `gate = Threshold(0.5)(far − can − primary food here)`; `gx = gate · dx`, `gy = gate · dy`; `Move(E) += gx`, `Move(W) −= gx`, `Move(S) += gy`, `Move(N) −= gy`: 4 compute nodes, 12 edges | 0.17 separates the four cardinal ring cells the founder reads (0.141 at vision radius 5) from the diagonal ring cells (0.2), which do not gate; weight 1.0 |
| `A_scalar` | `FoodHere(1)` | 0 | `fruit = Threshold(0)(fruit here)`; `fruit_only = Threshold(0.5)(fruit − primary food here)`; `Eat += fruit_only`, `EatFoodType += fruit`: 2 compute nodes, 5 edges | weights 1.0 |

`A_scalar` gates its `Eat` vote on fruit being the only food: with grass here
the founder already eats once, and an ungated vote made the controller eat
twice (a competence violation the fixture caught), so only the type changes.
The `A_vector` gate is the four cardinal ring cells and the here cell:
nearest food on a diagonal ring cell opens it and steers on both axes, and
barriers on the diagonals never hide a cardinal ring cell (checked through
production visibility). Limitation: the area summary reports the nearest
visible food only, so a diagonal ring cell occluded by two orthogonal
barriers is simply absent and the controller steers toward the next nearest
food; the founder does not read diagonal ring cells either.

## Assay bookkeeping

Births are creatures alive at a tick's end and absent at the previous tick's
end, by inherited lineage arm; a newborn killed within its birth tick has no
observable parent without a production seam and counts in
`unattributed_births` (`births_total` = every arm's births + unattributed);
each replicate row stores both.
Replicate lines reach the raw record in world/replicate order. The run is
marked incomplete, with its stop reason, when the wall cap passes during or
after the replicates or the summary exceeds 300 KB. Exposure draws one
`SmallRng` per sampling tick and advances it through the six `A`/`Z` arms in
arm order.

**Projection.** The pilot measures the sum of two sequential phases at ≤3
concurrent tasks each: the incumbent-generation phase (`W_inc`, 3 worlds × 1
task) and one replicate wave (`W_rep`, 3 worlds × 1 replicate = 1 wave of 3
at 3 threads); `P3 = W_inc + 1×W_rep = 391.483251 s`. No per-phase
instrumentation exists in `crates/v3-cli/src/opportunity.rs` (a single
`Instant::now()` measures the whole `execute()` call; confirmed by reading
the file — no cost-probe test exists for this module, unlike
`bench/input_use/tests.rs`), so `W_inc` and `W_rep` cannot be read
separately from the pilot alone; only their sum is measured. Because
`W_inc, W_rep ≥ 0` and `W_inc + W_rep = P3`, the target `Projection = W_inc +
8×W_rep` is bounded: `P3 ≤ Projection ≤ 8×P3` (the upper bound is reached
when `W_inc → 0`). Using the conservative (highest, never-underestimating)
bound: **Projection ≤ 8 × 391.483251 = 3,131.87 s**, which is under 6,480 s
(90% of the 7,200 s cap), so the full run was not stopped. This bound is
consistent with the actual measured full run (1,692.02 s, inside
`[391.5 s, 3,131.9 s]`) and with why the rerun (matched 3-thread concurrency
in pilot and full) did not reproduce the void run's overrun: the previous
8-thread pilot only ever ran 3 concurrent tasks (same structural bound as
this 3-thread pilot: 386.406 s at 8 threads vs 391.483 s at 3 threads, a
1.3% difference consistent with host-load noise, not thread count), while
the previous 8-thread full run's 8-way concurrency inflated per-task cost in
a way no ×8 pilot heuristic could see.
