## Options weighed at planning (2026-10-21)

Inputs: the T26 row and Notes (design note §3 D, review findings 5, 7
and 11), the T26.F01 spec's Goal, Inputs and Invariants and Performance

| Question | Options | Chosen, and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where the capacity lives | `HeritableBody.capacity: f32`; a field on `CreatureState` beside `energy`; an integer in energy units | The body record: F01 built it for exactly this, inheritance is one call, and `energy_cap` already reads `self`. `f32` keeps the cap comparison and the charges in the energy type; the record drops `Eq` and keeps `PartialEq`. |
| Mutation form | log-space uniform step `C' = C·e^δ`, δ ~ U[−s, s]; log-normal; additive step | Log-space uniform, one chance draw and one step draw: the design note names log space; a bounded uniform step is one draw and gives a symmetric ×/÷ walk whose extremes are known (e^0.25 = 1.284), and the clamp to [50, 800] needs no further rule. |
| Mutation gate | drawn only when `applied_events > 0` (the phenotype and kin-tag gate); drawn on every birth with its own chance | Every birth: a body trait that can only move with a brain mutation ties its rate to genome size and the engine's rate; F01 kept the phenotype gate as inherited coupling, not as a rule to extend. The phenotype-then-kin-tag order is untouched; the capacity draws come after. |
| Move factor placement | multiply the move base before `action_effort`'s noop floor; multiply the result after the complexity and age scaling | Before the floor: the noop floor is "every action costs at least a NoOp" (T16.F02) and must hold for a small body too; the complexity and age multipliers scale brain and age, not body, so the body factor belongs on the base. |
| Negative upkeep booking | three-way partition with decay and carrying booked as today and the signed remainder to `capacity_upkeep`; proportional split; net the upkeep into decay | Signed remainder: a neutral creature books byte-for-byte what it books today; a small body's saving appears as a negative `capacity_upkeep`, never as a smaller decay or carrying figure (the track's "without subsidizing either"); the zero floor's shortfall lands in the same bucket. |
| Newborn above capacity | cap at birth, excess to a new `offspring_capacity_overflow` bucket; refund the excess to the parent; raise the child's capacity to the litter | Cap and book: the parent at delivery may already sit at its own cap and a refund would need a second cap step; raising the capacity is a hidden inheritance path for the trait. The litter rule (fraction of the parent's energy, capped at 100, floored at 20) is unchanged. |
| Sensor denominator | the fixed reference capacity (200) for every energy read; the creature's own capacity; own capacity for self reads and the reference for nearby reads | Both implemented behind `energy.capacity.sensor_scale`, ruled by a predeclared rule after the unchanged-brain comparison (Codex round 1 F1: the design note §3 D places the ruling after the comparison; the planning draft had fixed the reference up front). | Frontend energy bar scale | the reference capacity; the global `max_energy` (800 after this feature); per-creature capacity in the detail-view payload | The reference, so the canvas looks as it does today; the inspector shows the creature's own capacity through the detail `max_energy` field F01 already routed through `energy_cap`. A per-creature payload field is not needed for a 2,000-tick reading. |
| Where β and u come from | a goal-profile sweep of scratch builds (the 2026-10-10 effort-cost pattern); a lab; the design note's guess | The sweep, as the track requires; its grid and its choice rule are predeclared in the spec so the pick is not post hoc. |
| Goal-summary reading | capacity distribution per persistence sample (trend) in `PersistenceSample`; a new goal indicator | A sample block: criterion 3 asks for distribution, boundary mass and trend per world, which the existing `SAMPLE_EVERY_TICKS` series gives for free; an indicator needs a direction and a threshold the track does not define. |
