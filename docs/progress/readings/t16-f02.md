### Frozen-count projection (planning only, not a prediction)

Every T11.F29 count held fixed and the new prices applied with the mean move
multiplier (move charges / (0.2 × moves): 1.0336 / 1.0434 / 1.0349) as the
multiplier proxy; blocked-move effort taken as the blocked share of move
charges. Reproduce attempts are unknown and excluded, so every "after" value is
a lower bound on the charge it projects. Under the new prices creatures act
differently; this is bookkeeping, as the action-choice note's Section 2.2 is.
