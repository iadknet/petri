## Self-review (simplify pass)

One change, committed as `b521b534`: `occupancy_grid` counted distinct
`(cell, lineage_id)` runs by carrying a `previous: Option<(usize, u32)>` through
the loop; it now walks `slice::chunk_by` over the sorted pairs, adding
`run.len()` to the cell's population and one to its clade count. Same values,
no carried state. Rejected as spec-pinned rather than cleanups: replacing the
sorted `Vec` with a hash structure (a `Decision:` bullet forbids hash order
reaching a stored report), clamping the cell index (pass 1 removed clamps
deliberately), dropping `cells_x`/`cells_y` from the wire type (the stored
report is required to carry them), and folding the `[u64; 256]` arrays into the
kernel's `Grid<T>` (serde derives no array above 32, and the fixed length is
the artifact's guarantee). The three cargo commands above were rerun after the
change.

## Stored-grid evidence (goal report)

Every checkpoint sample of all three world cases carries the grid, with
`cells_x == cells_y == 16`, both arrays at length 256, and the per-checkpoint
`population` array summing to that sample's own `population`.

| World case | Seed | Checkpoint samples | Samples carrying a grid | Samples passing all four checks | Horizon tick | Horizon population | Occupied cells at the horizon | Busiest cell | Most clades in one cell | Sum of per-cell clade counts | Surviving founder clades |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Orchards in grassland | 11 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 2,000 | 11,313 | 202 / 256 | 454 | 4 | 294 | 23 |
| Canyon country | 22 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 2,000 | 7,090 | 113 / 256 | 391 | 3 | 152 | 25 |
| Confluence | 33 | 20 | 20 | 20 | 2,000 | 9,995 | 160 / 256 | 357 | 3 | 227 | 17 |

Sixty checkpoint samples, sixty grids, zero failures. Canyon country occupies
113 of 256 cells at a population of 7,090 while Orchards occupies 202 at 11,313
— a difference in where life stands that a world total hides. The grid records
the occupancy; it says nothing about what produced it. The per-cell clade sums (294, 152, 227) exceed the
surviving founder clade counts (23, 25, 17) because a clade counts once in
every cell it occupies — clades are spread across cells rather than confined to
one.
