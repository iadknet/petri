## Options weighed at planning (2026-10-20)

| Question | Options | Chosen, and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where the stored value lives | absolute `learned` (today) with an ulp residual near `b`; the deviation `learned − b` with the clamp re-derived as `[−c − b, c − b]` | Absolute: the residual `ulp(x)/(2(1 − ρ))` (about `6e-5` at `ρ = 0.999`) is below the learning noise of every live rate, and storing the deviation touches every reader of `learned` (view, capture, reward, commit, applied). |
| The enable draw | `d = 10ᵘ`, `u ~ U[−4, 0]` (`powf`); a decade `gen_range(0..4)` plus a uniform mantissa (piecewise); a hand-written table of `10^(−k/10)` | `powf`: it is already in the tick loop (`runtime/cgp/eval.rs:39` sigmoid, `kernel/ordinary_food/grazing.rs:22`), so it adds no new platform dependence, and the law reads as the note states it. |
| The step law | `d ← d·(1 + 0.3u)` clamped (the catalog's `perturb` upper branch); `log10 d ← log10 d + 0.3u` (`powf` per target); an additive step on `ρ` | The multiplicative branch: it is the catalog's existing law on continuous attributes (`MutateHebbianRate`, `MutateTraceDecay`), a multiplicative step is a log-space step, and it needs no transcendental call per target. |
| Which edges the bins count | every reachable plastic edge by its stored `ρ`; only own-decaying learners (ungrouped or edge-scope) | Every plastic edge in `count`, with an `initialized` count beside it for the drift denominator (Codex round 1 A2): it mirrors `edges_by_inheritance`, which also counts a group learner's inert `lamarckian` flag, and the goal question is whether the mutation supply moves `ρ` at all; the scales table carries the group retention. |
| Bin edges | decades of `d` from the enable floor `10⁻⁴` up; exhaustive bins over the admitted `ρ ∈ [0, 1]` with the slowest bin open below `10⁻³` | Exhaustive: the roadmap admits every finite `ρ ∈ [0, 1]` and a loaded genome is not bound by a draw floor (Codex round 1 B2). |
| Operator count and weights | six (enable, disable, step per layer) at the tiers of `EnableRewardModulation` / `DisableRewardModulation` / `MutateTraceDecay` (1 / 1 / 4); the same six at 1 / 1 / 2; one state-dependent operator per layer | Six at 1 / 1 / 4: the track note names enable, disable and step operators, and the catalog's rule is "each the tier of the operator it parallels" (`graph/mod.rs` weight comments); one operator with a state-dependent law would couple three draws. | The census drift | `\|learned − genomic\|` (today); `\|learned − birth\|` | From birth: with the birth clamp, drift from the raw genomic weight would report a nonzero drift on an out-of-clamp slot before any learning (18–21% of plastic edges sat at their clamp in the F06 goal, seed 11); drift from birth is the quantity decay acts on. |
| `plasticity_scale_snaps` | keep (always zero after the birth clamp); delete | Delete: a counter with no reachable non-zero case is a dead reading kept for compatibility, which Petri does not keep. |
