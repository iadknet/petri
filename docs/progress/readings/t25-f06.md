Primary sources, read in full on 2026-10-18 (the design note's §5 asked the
F06 spec owner to read both; the claims it had rested on abstracts are
confirmed):

| Source | What it supports | What it does not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Miconi, Clune & Stanley 2018, *Differentiable plasticity* ([arXiv:1804.02464](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.02464), ar5iv text) | Each connection has a fixed weight and a plastic component scaled by its own coefficient α (Eq. 1); a connection is fixed (α = 0), fully plastic (w = 0) or mixed. Independent per-connection α beats one shared α on image memorization (Fig. 5) and maze exploration (§4.5, where shared-α networks "settle on a low performance plateau"): the per-edge decision over the per-node one. Oja's rule (Eq. 3) keeps weights bounded without a decay term where the simple rule (Eq. 2) needs decay against runaway. | Their η is one shared scalar "for simplicity"; Hebbian traces reset to zero each lifetime, so Lamarckian inheritance of learned weights is Petri's choice, not theirs. |
| Gerstner, Lehmann, Liakoni, Corneil & Brea 2018, *Eligibility traces and plasticity on behavioral time scales* (Front. Neural Circuits 12:53, [PMC6079224](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6079224/)) | Eq. 1: the flag `de/dt = η f_j(x_j) g_i(y_i) − e/τ_e` is set by pre **and** post co-activation, never pre alone (§3.3, §4.3), and its functions carry indices "because the rules can depend on the type of pre- and postsynaptic neurons": rule per synapse type. Eq. 2: `dw/dt = e · M3rd(t)`, a global signed third factor that sets speed and direction; Type 2 rules (Eq. 3) give each postsynaptic site its own gain on the global factor — Petri's per-edge choice of one of four creature-global channels. `τ_e` should match the delay from action to reward (200 ms–2 s for an elementary action, §2.3.1) and "could vary from one brain area to the next" (§4.4): an evolvable per-edge decay is the mechanism's own degree of freedom. The third factor converts the flag; it does not clear it. | Continuous time; Petri's tick is the unit and `trace_decay` the per-tick retention. The paper does not speak to clamps or to learning on an output (vote) synapse; it does model state→action synapses (§2.3.1), which is what a vote edge is. |
