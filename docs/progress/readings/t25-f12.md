## Research summary (Plan, 2026-10-28)

The model is the user's (track Notes, 2026-10-22; the
[bundle learning note](../../strategy/bundle-learning-ownership-2026-10-22.md));
no external library or standard applies to an in-house genome representation,
so the research is local: the design note, the T25.F06–F11 specs and the code.
Choices the note left to the spec owner, with the alternatives considered:

| Choice | Options | Chosen | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slot birth draw | fair presence coin then a config; F07's `draw_mode` (absent, Hebbian, modulated, a third each) | `draw_mode` | one birth law for every `Learning` slot, member or bundle; no new law |
| Member policy birth draw | three-way `gen_range(0..3)`; inherit coin then `draw_mode` | inherit coin then `draw_mode` | the note's fair-coin default for the new choice; keeps F07's mode law for the rest; the learning-member share stays 2/3 where `member_default` is present |
| `credits` birth draw | fair coin; derived from policy | fair coin, drawn whatever the slots | the note's default; neutral variation for a later `scale_learning` |
| Learning-edit target draw | domain draw over target kinds; one uniform draw over every eligible target | one uniform draw, edges first then bundle slots | today's count-then-select law extended, no new weights |
| Policy transitions | enable/disable only on `Own`; three setters each from the other two policies | three setters (`Enable` → `Own`, `Disable` → `Static`, `ClearLearningOverride` → `Inherit`) | every transition one event, by the maximum-flexibility rule |
| Bundle retention operators | keep three bundle operators; fold into the edge retention operators over every `Learning` | fold | one operator per edit (audit R4) |
| Update count | per contribution (today); per learner update | per learner update | F07 invariant 9 defines updates as learner assignments; a scale is one learner |
