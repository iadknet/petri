## Wall-clock delta, and how much of it is the census

The predeclaration expects a small compute cost, larger than T14.F04's. The
measured wall clock crossed the `flag` threshold on both profiles and stayed
below `severe`:

| Reading | T14.F01 | T14.F04 | T14.F08 | vs T14.F04 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gate wall total | 594.73 ms | 540.86 ms | 1,009.15 ms | +86.6 % |
| Gate ms per creature-tick | 0.001391 | 0.001265 | 0.002360 | +86.6 % |
| Goal wall total | — | 460,451.08 ms | 636,820.52 ms | +38.3 % |
| Goal ms per creature-tick | 0.008082 (T12.F04) | 0.007552 | 0.010445 | +38.3 % |

Most of that delta is not attributable to the census. The census runs only
inside checkpoint sampling, which is outside the five timed tick phases and
outside the neighborhood batteries and the drift walk entirely — yet those
untouched measurements rose by the same order:

| Measurement, census cannot run in it | T14.F04 | T14.F08 | Delta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal `neighborhood_founder_wall_clock_ms` | 97.82 ms | 230.65 ms | +135.8 % |
| Goal `neighborhood_evolved_wall_clock_ms_total` | 426.49 ms | 659.74 ms | +54.7 % |
| Goal `drift_depth_wall_clock_ms` | 18,170.11 ms | 25,719.08 ms | +41.5 % |

sensor_assembly + world_update`) against the rest of the loop, which is where
checkpoint sampling and the census sit:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | T14.F04 | 192.26 | 179.94 | 12.32 |

The remainder grew by 14.0, 13.9 and 17.7 ms per seed — an upper bound on the
census's cost, since the remainder also covers the rest of the sampling. The
timed phases, which contain no census work at all, grew by 105, 111 and 207 ms
per seed over the same runs. The host was under concurrent load during this
pass, so the flagged percentages describe the machine more than the feature.
Each profile is run once per closure, so no rerun was taken to separate them
further; the census's own share is bounded at roughly 15 ms per gate seed.
