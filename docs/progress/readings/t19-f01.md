recorded in `TickComputeStats::record` (`crates/v3-core/src/simulation/tick.rs`)
and read every 500 ticks by a throwaway `v3-cli` integration test that built
each goal world exactly as the goal profile does (`resolve_config` over the
checked-in recipe, then `build_config(goal_profile_params())`) and ran it 2,000
ticks with `run_tick`. Code at `437e3358`, release build, host Apple M1 Pro,
2026-09-21. The probe was reverted after the run; nothing of it is committed.

## Ramp cost at the chosen constants (`hop_ramp_allowance` 32, `hop_ramp_cost` 1e-4)

Per tick, `n` hops cost `1e-4 * m * (m + 1) / 2` with `m = max(0, n - 32)`.

| Hops in one tick | Ramp energy | Against `initial_energy` 20 and goal mean energy ~32 |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 9 (goal maximum) | 0 | nothing paid |
| 32 | 0 | nothing paid |
| 48 | 0.0136 | one long pass, survivable |
| 64 | 0.0528 | one capped pass at a 64-hop cap, survivable |
| 128 | 0.4656 | 1.5% of initial energy |
| 352 (11 passes of 32) | 5.136 | lethal within about four to six ticks of sustained cycling |
| 704 (11 passes of 64) | 22.628 | lethal in one to two ticks |
| 1,024 (`max_mesh_hops` today) | 49.253 | lethal in one tick; T03.F10's VM ramp costs about 49 at its own cap |
