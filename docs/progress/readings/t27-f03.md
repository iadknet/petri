## Options weighed at planning (2026-10-21)

Fixed by the user's 2026-10-21 decisions and not reopened: a heritable share
per kind in `HeritableBody` (founder near 0.2, cryptic until wired), an
effort field per kind that is neutral when unwired, amount = share × modifier
× the source's current energy, graded effort for give as for steal, a zero
transfer as a failure with no kill credit, no fixed global amount.

| Question | Options | Chosen, and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share mutation form | (a) log-space uniform step `s' = s·e^δ`, δ ~ U[−σ, σ], clamped to `[share_min, 1]`, as T26.F02's capacity; (b) logit-space step (no clamp needed); (c) additive step with T11.F23's `max(|c|, 1)` floor | (a): one chance draw and one step draw per kind, the same rule and config type as the capacity, so the body has one scalar-inheritance rule instead of two; a relative step keeps a small share small and a large one large, which the row asks for. (c) is absolute across (0, 1] (design review finding 2). (b) is a second rule for no gain: the clamp at 1 is a plateau the share can leave by the next downward step. |
| Share range | `[0.01, 1]`; `(0, 1]` with no floor; `[0.05, 0.5]` | `[exchange.share_min = 0.01, 1]`: a floor keeps the share from walking into sub-resolution bites that always fail (a creature that cannot act is not "cryptic"); 1 is "take everything", which is the only way a steal kills under the share form. The bounds are config like `energy.capacity.min`, not amounts. |
| Share mutation config | one `mutation.share` for both kinds; `mutation.steal_share` and `mutation.give_share` | one: the two shares are the same kind of trait; a per-kind rate has no hypothesis behind it. |
| Effort modifier shape | (a) `2^clamp(x, −2, 2)` → `[0.25, 4]`; (b) `max(0, 1 + x)` clamped at 2; (c) `exp(x)` unclamped | (a): never negative, exactly ×1 at the unwired 0 and at NaN, symmetric in log space (a weight of ±1 doubles or halves), bounded so a saturated node cannot request ∞; (b) can reach 0 (an attempt that pays and can never move anything) and is asymmetric; (c) is unbounded. |
| Intensity cap | `min(1, share × modifier)`; let `share × modifier` exceed 1 and clamp the amount to the source's energy | `min(1, …)`: the amount is a fraction of the source, so the fraction is what is bounded; the charge base then also stays bounded by the actor's own energy. |
| Charge base (the row leaves it to the spec) | (a) `effort_rate × intensity × actor.energy` for both acts; (b) `effort_rate × intensity × victim.energy` for steal (the floor when there is none); (c) `effort_rate × intensity × capacity`; (d) the floor only | (a): one rule for both acts; for give it is today's steal form (rate × the amount attempted) exactly; for steal the attacker's own store stands in for the unknown victim's, so the charge is known before the target resolves and does not leak whether a victim exists; graded as the row requires. (b) charges by outcome; (c) ignores the state factor the row names; (d) is not graded. |
| Where the effort rate lives | keep `predation.steal_cost_rate`; `exchange.effort_rate` with `PredationConfig` keeping the kill bonus | `exchange.effort_rate`: give would otherwise be governed by a field named for steal in a block named predation; the reverted feature already introduced an `exchange` block; no backward compatibility is owed. |
| Zero-transfer result keys | map both zero cases to `RejectedNoVictim`; a distinct `RejectedNothingToTake` | distinct: the row's zero-transfer finding (kills outnumbering energy taken) needs the exhausted-victim count visible in `predation_actions_by_result`; one enum arm and one key. |
| `GiveEnergy` order in the catalog | after `StealEnergy`; before it | after: appending keeps the three existing kinds' indices and the `RetargetGroupKind` complement rule unchanged in meaning. |
| Share exposure | creature detail `steal_share`, `give_share`; a goal-summary distribution like `capacity`; both | both: the detail is one field each; the goal summary gains a `shares` block from the pass that already samples the capacity, so the 2,000-tick reading can say whether the shares moved while cryptic. |

### Payoff predeclaration arithmetic (spec Performance section)

At neutral effort (modifier 1), founder share 0.2, intensity 0.2, effort
rate 0.05, floor 0.4, multiplier m ≥ 1 (`action_cost_multiplier`), population
mean energy E in 24–33 (T27.F01 goal readings), blind victim-hit rate h in
4–9% (design review finding 4):

| Quantity | Formula | Value at the defaults |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charge base | `0.05 × 0.2 × E_actor` | 0.24–0.33, below the floor: the charge is `0.4 × m` |
| Base above the floor when | `intensity × E_actor > 8` | intensity 1 and energy > 8; intensity 0.2 and energy > 40 |
| Steal gross, blind | `h × 0.2 × E_victim` | 0.19–0.59 per attempt |
| Steal gross, aimed (h = 1) | `0.2 × E_victim` | 4.8–6.6 per attempt, before the attacker's cap |
| Break-even hit rate | `0.4 × m / (0.2 × E_victim)` | 6–8% at m = 1 |
| Give, giver's side | `−(0.4 × m + 0.2 × E_giver)` | −(5.2–7.0) |
| Give, recipient's side | `0.2 × E_giver − clamp loss` | up to 4.8–6.6 |
| Kill by steal | the whole store taken: intensity 1 (`share × modifier ≥ 1`), or `f32` rounding of `i × V.energy` up to `V.energy` on a store of a few ulps | share ≥ 0.25 at modifier 4, or share 1 at neutral; at the founder's 0.2 × 1 only the rounding case |
