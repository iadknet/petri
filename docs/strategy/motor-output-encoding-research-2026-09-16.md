# Motor Output Encoding Research Note (2026-09-16)

Companion to the [live survey](live-survey-2026-09-16.md), which found that Petri's creatures steer by direction constants, evolved exactly one sensor-to-direction rule (fruit at West, move West) in 400 generations, and never avoid barriers, and traced the cause to the scalar direction decode. This note asks what other evolutionary systems do about the same problem and grounds the roadmap feature T11.F21.

Research was a 103-agent deep-research run (5 search angles, 21 sources fetched, 3-vote adversarial verification, 1 claim refuted) on 2026-09-16, plus the local code reading. The biology angle (motor maps, basal ganglia selection, chemotaxis) returned no claim that survived verification, so the analog below is named from general knowledge and not cited.

## Problem, constraints, decision criteria

**Problem.** `runtime/action_decode.rs::decode_direction` turns one scalar parameter into a compass index by `round(x).clamp(0, 7)`; an unwired parameter is 0 = North. A brain that wants to move toward the best of eight neighbor cells must compute an index-of-maximum, about seven comparisons and seven selects with constants 0 to 7, and no partial construction is better than the constant it replaces. Barrier avoidance for one slot is cheaper (one negative gate edge) and is also absent.

**Constraints.** The T11 contract: a change must be neutral at birth (an existing genome behaves exactly as before), founder readings must not move, and a new form must be reachable by the existing operator families in one or two events. The natural-analog rule: a mechanism reaches creatures through the body, not through a sensor invented for it. Both backends (VM programs and CGP graphs) must have the same opportunity (T11.F18).

**Criteria.** (1) Is a single sensor-to-direction reflex one selectable step? (2) Is the form neutral at birth and founder-preserving? (3) Precedent in evolved systems, with any evidence on evolvability. (4) Cost in operators, config, and report surface.

## Material findings

1. **No named system decodes a direction from a rounded scalar.** The four families found are per-action winner-take-all, continuous signed scalars per motor axis, egocentric relative turns, and factored discrete action spaces. Sources: Kelly and Heywood 2017 (TPG), Kelly et al. 2021, Lichodzijewski and Heywood 2008 (SBB); Hausknecht et al. 2014 (Atari neuroevolution); Yaeger 1994 (Polyworld); Sims 1994; Framsticks documentation; Grabowski et al. 2013 (Avida); Wilson and Kaur 2013 (Santa Fe).
2. **Bid-based GP is the direct structural precedent for option A and states its rationale**: "the bid-based approach makes the decomposition explicit and simplifies credit assignment, i.e., variation can be made specific to a single program-action pairing" (Kelly et al. 2021), with an eight-direction joystick as the illustrative action set. The evolved program never computes the action index; the argmax lives in the substrate. Confidence high; design rationale, not a controlled comparison.
3. **Neuroevolution uses one output per action and argmax, never a scalar index** (Hausknecht et al. 2014: CNE, CMA-ES, NEAT with a 1xA output layer; HyperNEAT with a geometric 3x3 direction substrate read by highest activation). Confidence high; no comparison against a scalar alternative was run.
4. **Under continuous per-axis encodings a single sensor-to-effector edge is a working reflex** (Sims 1994: an effector "simply contains a connection to a neuron or a sensor", and following becomes fleeing by sign negation). Confidence high; architectural inference, evolved circuits were not analyzed at edge level.
5. **Encoding changes learnability in gradient RL**: on a get-to-goal task, per-direction discrete buttons learned fastest, tank-style turns slower, a single continuous scalar angle slowest or not at all; discretizing the scalar restored learning (Kanervisto, Scheller and Hautamaki 2020). Confidence medium; PPO, not evolution, and the scalar condition is an angle, not a round-and-clamp index.
6. **Representation change within one family produced the only large measured evolvability gain**: the Santa Fe Ifc-ADF redesign raised success from 10.6% to 72.7% and cut effort from 450k to 67k evaluations (Wilson and Kaur 2013, unrefereed preprint, literature baseline). Confidence medium.
7. **Encoding is not the sole cause of constant-direction outcomes**: Polyworld's first species ran straight or spun regardless of vision under a continuous encoding (Yaeger 1994), and vision-guided orbiting evolved later. Confidence medium (2 to 1 vote; behavioral description, no circuit analysis).
8. **Local code facts.** The VM writes actions through an eight-slot `meta` array (`WriteWorldActionMeta { slot_idx < 8 }`) of which `decode_world_action` reads two and the ISA reserves the rest, so a bank needs its own buffer rather than the six spare slots; the graph `ActionSlot` has `gate_inputs` and `param_inputs` and its parameter is `input x weight`, unwired = 0; `runtime/action_decode.rs` is the one place both backends decode, so one rule there serves both.

## Recommendation

**Build option A, per-direction winner-take-all, as T11.F21**, with option B's vector sensors covered by the same bank (a gradient component is one more edge into a bid, not a second form). Against the strongest alternative, B: A is one selectable step for every sensor the population already references (the rings are referenced in 131 to 283 of 400 genomes and consumed by two slots), B is one step only for the area summaries, and A has the closer precedent in the evolved-program literature. C is rejected on neutrality and on the evidence. D is rejected because it leaves the VM behind and is still not one edge.

## Contract for T11.F21 (decisions the spec owner works from)

Read with the row in the T11 track. Anything not listed here is the spec owner's call within the T11 node-type contract.

1. **Bank.** A movement action (`Move`, `Reproduce`, `StealEnergy`) may carry a direction bank: eight bids, one per `Direction::ALL` index. Bids are weighted sums; a bank slot with nothing wired has bid 0.
2. **Decode rule** (shared by both backends, in `runtime/action_decode.rs` beside `decode_direction`): if no bank slot was written for this action, the scalar decode applies unchanged. Otherwise the direction is the index of the maximum bid; when several slots tie at the maximum, the scalar-decoded direction wins if it is among them, else the lowest tied index. Consequences the spec relies on: one positive edge `food[d] -> bid d` changes nothing while `food[d]` is 0 (all bids tie, scalar decode wins) and selects d when it is positive; one negative edge `barrier[d] -> bid d` alone never selects d and otherwise leaves the scalar decode in force. That is the one-edge property and the neutrality guarantee in one rule.
3. **Graph form.** `ActionSlot` gains a serde-defaulted `direction_bids: Vec<DirectionBidEdge>` (a `GraphEdge` plus a `direction: u8`), evaluated in the same effects pass as `param_inputs`; `Pop` and `Eat` slots ignore it. It is a sixth edge-bearing surface for `AddGraphEdge`, `RemoveGraphEdge`, `RetargetGraphEdge`, and `AlterGraphEdgeWeight`; the source draw, applicability rules, and the split exclusion in `v3-mutation-spec.md` apply as they do to `param_inputs`. `GraphActionSlotTrace` gains the eight bids and the chosen index when the bank was written.
4. **VM form.** One opcode, `WriteDirectionBid { direction: u8, src }`, writing a per-dispatch eight-slot bid buffer beside `meta` and marking the bank written; `PushAction` for a movement type applies rule 2; the buffer and flag reset with `meta` at node end; out-of-range `direction` is ignored like an out-of-range meta slot. Opcode cost is set beside `WriteWorldActionMeta` in the ISA cost table. It enters programs through the existing insertion and operand operators (T11.F02 semantics); no new operator family.
5. **Neutral at birth.** Founder programs and graphs write no bank, all stored genomes deserialize with empty banks, and the decode is bit-identical for them, so founder rows, the gate trajectory, and every T11.F14 founder reading are unchanged. The goal trajectory is expected to diverge once evolved genomes write banks; a goal-epoch re-pin at closure is the predeclared outcome, not a regression.
6. **Readings.** A `steering-v1` battery separate from `neighborhood-v1` (which is not edited): from zeroed memory and fresh graph state as T11.F14 does, (a) food only at direction d for each d with no food here, over a fixed base set, giving the exact-hit and within-45-degree fractions of moves; (b) for every base and d where the genome leads with `Move(d0)`, the same base with `barrier[d0] = 1`, giving the avoidance fraction; (c) the fraction of sampled genomes whose executed nodes write a bank (structural, from the mesh annotations). Stored beside the T11.F14 mesh-execution block on the same sample: founder half in the gate report, evolved half in the goal report. Chance levels 0.125 and 0.375 are printed beside (a). The 2026-09-16 survey probe (Appendix B of the live survey note) is the reference implementation of (a) and (b).
7. **Predeclared directions.** Evolved half: exact-hit above 0.125 and avoidance above 0; bank-written fraction above 0. Founder half: identical to the pre-feature reading. Neighborhood indicator: changed-per-birth not down, dead-per-birth not up. If (a) and (b) stay at chance while (c) is above 0, record finding 7 (ecology) as the explanation; the feature still closes on the founder and neutrality readings.
8. **Out of scope, recorded as follow-ons.** Topographic operator bias (an edge from ring slot d preferring bank slot d); the same bank form for the `Eat` food-type parameter when a third food type lands; inspector rendering of the bids (the trace carries them; `docs/progress` and the frontend are unchanged); any sensor change.

The analog is a motor map with winner-take-all selection: a population of direction-tuned outputs competing, the way a superior colliculus map or basal ganglia selection commits one movement; the eight neighbor cells are the map. This reaches creatures through the body's output, not through a new sensor.

**Remaining trade-off.** Finding 7: a better encoding removes the representational block, not the ecological one. If a fixed diagonal harvest stays the best strategy in a wrap world with 54% food coverage, seeking may stay rare even when it is one edge away; the feature's readings are designed to tell those apart.

## Remaining uncertainty and the cheap proof

- No source runs an evolutionary head-to-head of scalar-index decoding against per-direction WTA; the case is analogy (findings 2 to 5) plus the survey's mechanism trace.
- The cheap proof, in the T11.F15 pattern: a scratch-worktree counterfactual on one goal-profile seed with the bank in place, read with the survey's paired-perturbation probe (exact-hit fraction on food-at-d, barrier-avoidance fraction) on the evolved half. Predeclared: exact hits above 12.5% on the evolved half, avoidance above 0, founder rows unchanged, changed-per-birth not down. If both readings stay at chance with the bank present, finding 7 is the explanation and the feature's value is in the reading, not the mechanism.
