# Diverse Ecosystems and Complex Cognition

**Status**: Active
**Last updated**: 2026-09-04

## Success Definition

Petri succeeds when its world feels alive: long-running seeded runs sustain
many coexisting ways of making a living, and lineages keep evolving behavior
that depends on remembering, learning, and predicting rather than reacting
alone. Progress is tracked, not proved. A small set of diversity and cognition
indicators, read on one standard goal profile at the close of every feature,
rises across the series of closed features, and each rise is attributed to the
feature whose closure moved it.

## Final Success Criteria

- [ ] The goal profile's ecological-diversity indicators (surviving lineage count and lineage entropy first, richer strategy measures as they are added) rise across the series of closed features relative to the goal series' first reading, and each rise survives at least one later feature.
- [ ] The goal profile's cognition indicators (memory sensitivity first, learning and prediction measures as they are added) rise across the series of closed features relative to the goal series' first reading, and each rise survives at least one later feature.
- [ ] Every closed feature carries a stored benchmark report, no severe unjustified compute regression is present in the series, and the gate profile still finishes inside `make check`.
- [ ] Seeded runs reproduce their trajectory byte-for-byte across processes and thread counts at every world size the goal profile uses.
- [ ] `docs/progress.md` shows one row per closed feature with its date, compute delta, and every indicator reading, so program direction is visible from Git alone.
- [ ] Every mechanism feature names its natural analog in its roadmap goal line and reaches creatures through the world and body rather than through a sensor invented for the feature.

- Evidence classes. Goal-profile readings are the program's success evidence: the series across closed features advances the final criteria above. A single reading is not proof, and no feature may optimize against an indicator; a feature is judged by the mechanism it adds, and the indicator only records what that mechanism did. The causal-proof apparatus (result-blind protocols, campaigns, result bundles) is a deferred later phase and is not required by any criterion above.

When that phase runs, its rules still hold: every experiment is labeled fixture, characterization, or confirmatory in its manifest; confirmatory campaigns depend on T01.F09 and use its result-blind frozen protocol; exposure is bounded in births, mutation opportunities, lineage depth, ticks, and wall-clock rather than ticks alone; campaigns use an explicit repository-external artifact root with manifest-declared wall-clock, concurrency, and storage caps, and exceeding a cap requires renewed user authorization; numerical thresholds are frozen by T01.F09 from predeclared rules before any confirmatory outcome is generated. Long campaigns establish emergence and persistence; short paired replays establish causal mechanisms.
- Every feature closed after T10.F10 stores a compact benchmark report with two halves measured on two profiles. The gate profile is seconds-scale, runs inside `make check`, and carries the compute-performance half: a severe compute regression against either the previous closed feature or the pinned epoch baseline blocks closure unless the feature predeclared and justified the cost, in which case its closing commit re-pins the epoch baseline. The goal profile, added by T01.F12, is minutes-scale, runs once at closure through `make bench`, is checked for determinism by a second run at closure rather than inside `make check`, and carries the goal-indicator half. Any feature that introduces a diversity or cognition measure wires its indicator into the goal profile as part of the same feature. Neither half is a dashboard, a database, or a composite score.
- T08.F06 through T08.F09 (mating, recombination, and the heredity-mode campaign) remain later work after foundation diagnostics and mutation evidence are reviewed. T08.F06 carries the diagnostic and heredity prerequisites; no early foundation or ecological mechanism depends on sexual reproduction.
- Energy is ecological accounting, not a standalone track or a direct complexity reward. Every new capacity must have an applied cost or tradeoff owned by the feature that introduces it.
- Every mechanism feature is grounded in a natural analog and works toward a world that feels alive and organic. Its roadmap goal line names the analog in a few words (seasons, terrain blocks sight, pheromone trails, a grazed patch recovers slowly); a feature that cannot name one in a sentence is a game mechanic and does not belong. The analog reaches creatures the way it does in nature, through the world and the body (scarce food, higher metabolic cost, a lost meal, a wound), never through a sensor invented for the feature (no season flag, no threat score). Clocks, physics, and creature actions are all natural sources of change; an authored fixed script is an experimental treatment, off in the production world. Measurement features are the microscope, not the world, and are exempt.
- T08 owns mutation, duplication, and inheritance integrity; T09 owns brain execution, temporal state, learning, and cognition diagnostics. Keep these responsibilities in the existing tracks. Basic engineering checks and demonstrated correctness repairs do not depend on the deferred causal-proof phase. Qualify the current substrate before selecting another controller architecture; the six new foundation features are bounded responses to inspected code, not a promise that cognition will emerge.
- Options considered were a richer fixed environment, an external curriculum, direct novelty or complexity rewards, and endogenous ecological feedback. The roadmap uses bounded fixed environments as assays and endogenous feedback as the long-term pressure because direct proxies can reward bloat or indefinite but trivial change.
