# Diverse Ecosystems and Complex Cognition

**Status**: Active
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Success Definition

Petri succeeds when its world feels alive: long-running seeded runs sustain
many coexisting ways of making a living, and lineages keep evolving behavior
that depends on remembering, learning, and predicting rather than reacting
alone. Progress is tracked, not proved. A small set of diversity and cognition
indicators, read on one standard goal profile at the close of every feature,
rises across the series of closed features, and each rise is attributed to the
feature whose closure moved it.

## Final Success Criteria

- [ ] The goal profile's ecological-diversity indicators (surviving lineage count and lineage entropy first, richer strategy measures as they are added) rise across the series of closed features relative to the goal series' first reading, and each rise survives at least one later feature.
- [ ] The goal profile's cognition indicators (memory sensitivity first, learning and prediction measures as they are added) rise across the series of closed features relative to the goal series' first reading, and each rise survives at least one later feature.
- [ ] Every closed feature carries a stored benchmark report, no severe unjustified compute regression is present in the series, and the gate profile still finishes inside `make check`.
- [ ] The mutational-neighborhood indicator reaches the floors T11 predeclares by the close of T11.F10, and from T11.F01 onward no closed feature lowers an indicator component below the previous closed feature's reading except where the closing spec predeclared and justified the change.
- [ ] Seeded runs reproduce their trajectory byte-for-byte across processes and thread counts at every world size the goal profile uses.
- [ ] `docs/progress.md` shows one row per closed feature with its date, compute delta, and every indicator reading, so program direction is visible from Git alone.
- [ ] Every mechanism feature names its natural analog in its roadmap goal line and reaches creatures through the world and body rather than through a sensor invented for the feature.

A single reading is not proof, and no feature may optimize against an indicator; a feature is judged by the mechanism it adds, and the indicator only records what that mechanism did. The causal-proof apparatus (result-blind protocols, campaigns, result bundles) is a deferred later phase and is not required by any criterion above.

Long campaigns establish emergence and persistence; short paired replays establish causal mechanisms.
- Every feature closed after T10.F10 stores a compact benchmark report with two halves measured on two profiles. The gate profile is seconds-scale, runs inside `make check`, and carries the compute-performance half: a severe compute regression against either the previous closed feature or the pinned epoch baseline blocks closure unless the feature predeclared and justified the cost, in which case its closing commit re-pins the epoch baseline. The goal profile, added by T01.F12, is minutes-scale, runs once at closure through `make bench`, and carries the goal-indicator half; a second determinism run is not required (see `docs/workflow.md`). Any feature that introduces a diversity or cognition measure wires its indicator into the goal profile as part of the same feature. Neither half is a dashboard, a database, or a composite score.
- T08.F06 through T08.F09 (mating, recombination, and the heredity-mode campaign) remain later work after the T11 foundation features and the T08 heredity evidence are reviewed. T08.F06 carries the diagnostic and heredity prerequisites; no early foundation or ecological mechanism depends on sexual reproduction.
- Energy is ecological accounting, not a standalone track or a direct complexity reward. Every new capacity must have an applied cost or tradeoff owned by the feature that introduces it.
- Every mechanism feature is grounded in a natural analog and works toward a world that feels alive and organic. Its roadmap goal line names the analog in a few words (seasons, terrain blocks sight, pheromone trails, a grazed patch recovers slowly); a feature that cannot name one in a sentence is a game mechanic and does not belong. The analog reaches creatures the way it does in nature, through the world and the body (scarce food, higher metabolic cost, a lost meal, a wound), never through a sensor invented for the feature (no season flag, no threat score). Clocks, physics, and creature actions are all natural sources of change; an authored fixed script is an experimental treatment, off in the production world. Measurement features are the microscope, not the world, and are exempt.

T08 owns lineage attribution, heritable mutation policy, mating, and recombination. T09 owns cognition assays, learning qualification, and the diagnostic checkpoint. - Brain evolvability audit 2026-09-04: [companion document](strategy/brain-evolvability-audit-2026-09-04.md), the evidence base for T11. Two in-process probes on the V3Alpha1 founder at production defaults (commit 4b8650ef) found that 90.2% of births are clones and, among mutated births, 5.9% are silent on a 48-scenario reactive battery, 75.4% changed, and 18.7% dead; single-event births are 32.4% silent and 4.1% dead. Causes isolated in code: relative jump offsets never recomputed on VM splices (one Noop insertion is 28.8% silent today and 100% with offsets recomputed), whole-instruction operand redraws, register-count aliasing, a graph add-node that sprays about 30 random edges (1.0% silent; 100% without the spray), no neutral scaffold in the founder, and a 10% mutation trigger followed by a uniform draw of 1 to 10 events. The research basis and the options considered are in the T11 notes; the earlier foundation review ([SignalGP module regulation](https://mmore500.com/pubs/lalejini2021tag), [NEAT](https://nn.cs.utexas.edu/downloads/papers/stanley.ec02.pdf), [The Evolutionary Origin of Associative Learning](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/706252)) is carried there. T01.F12's goal-v1 baseline (read 2026-09-04, verified 2026-09-05) corroborates the audit on evolved populations: zero of 24,418 final creatures across three seeds changed their action queue when shared memory was zeroed or rotated, while reachable structure grew to a median of 97, a p75 of 124, and a maximum of 798 against the founder's 96. The cited large-scale ecology study uses memoryless controllers and supplies a scale reference, not evidence of learning.
